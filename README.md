# Restock — monthly expenses & purchase reminders

A small, installable app (PWA) for things you buy again and again — every 2 days, weekly, monthly, every 2 months, or any interval — that reminds you before they're due and tracks what you spend.

## Features
- **Any repeat interval**: every N days / weeks / months, with one-tap presets (daily, 2 days, weekly, monthly, 2 months…).
- **Home dashboard**: overdue, due today, next 7 days and later; spent this month, end-of-month forecast, average monthly cost.
- **One tap "✓ Bought"**: records the purchase (price, quantity, date) and schedules the next one automatically. Snooze, skip, or undo.
- **Shopping list**: everything due today / this week / this month, grouped by store, tick off and mark all bought at once, share to WhatsApp/SMS.
- **Budget**: monthly budget with progress bar and "over budget" forecast warning.
- **Spending stats**: last 6 months, spend by category, planned monthly cost per category, top items, per-item price history and your *actual* buying rhythm.
- **Reminders**: browser notifications + app icon badge, and **calendar export (.ics)** with alarms — the most reliable option, works even when the app is closed.
- One-off expenses, quick-start list of common items, search & category filters, dark mode, works offline.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Backup/restore (JSON) and CSV export of purchases.

## Use it
Open `index.html` through any static web host (e.g. GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch). On your phone, open the site and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install it.

Local test: `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
